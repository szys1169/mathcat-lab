# Direct proof, reduction and transfer

Start with the strongest concrete mathematical observation available. Expand definitions when similarly named properties could differ. Distinguish equivalence, necessity and sufficiency in every reduction; preserve domains and quantifier order. Track whether normalization loses degenerate or boundary cases.

For a similar theorem, inspect how its proof obtains the needed construction, compactness, estimate or invariant. Transfer that mechanism only after checking its hypotheses in the new setting. A stronger conclusion in a cited paper is not usable if its assumptions are stronger too. State the exact residual lemma if the transfer stalls.

Write the actual deduction rather than a list of techniques that might work. You may pursue a whole argument before decomposing it. Missing intermediate claims stay conditional until justified; no fixed number of proof attempts is required.
