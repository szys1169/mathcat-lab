# Examples and counterexamples

Use hand-derived toy objects or special cases to isolate the role of an assumption. Check empty, trivial, singular, boundary and infinite cases when relevant, without treating this as a compulsory checklist for every problem.

A counterexample must satisfy every hypothesis and violate the exact conclusion. A counterexample to an intermediate assertion does not necessarily refute the original theorem. Verify the object exists and the definitions agree. Distinguish a concrete counterexample, a suspected obstruction and failure to find an example.

When a route repeatedly stalls, ask whether its missing lemma is false, too strong, or merely unproved. Preserve the useful weaker statement and indicate what changed. No enumeration or mathematical computation tool is part of this package.
